A secluded estate in the

Veluwe

The Welna Estate is a secluded, centuries-old estate nestled in one of the most pristine forests of the Veluwe, the Netherlands' largest and wildest nature reserve. With over 600 hectares of rolling woodland, ancient trees, and quiet paths, it offers a rare sense of stillness and depth.

What makes Welna truly special is its untouched atmosphere: no cars, no noise, just the pure presence of nature. It’s a place where time slows down, where you can feel the wisdom of the land, and where the forest gently invites you back to yourself.

Good to know

before you come
I have no camping experience. Can I still join?

Yes, absolutely. You can bring your own gear, rent it or borrow it, and we help you set up your tent.

What should I bring?

Camping gear, waterproof clothing, sturdy walking shoes or boots, a notebook and pen, and a refillable water bottle. Meals and drinks are provided. A full packing list follows when you sign up.

How fit do I need to be?

No special fitness is needed. It helps if you can walk 1 to 2 km and sit outdoors comfortably. Some paths are uneven, and a lightweight stool or cushion is handy if sitting on the ground is hard.

How big is the group?

Ten people at most. The retreat goes ahead with a minimum of five.

Do I need to bring my own food?

No. Meals, snacks and drinks are included, and we can take dietary needs into account.

What kind of food will we eat?

Fresh, seasonal, plant-based meals.

Are there showers and toilets?

Facilities are simple: one shared toilet and one shower with hot water for the whole group. It is part of the back-to-basics experience.

Should I worry about wildlife?

Not really. Wild animals sometimes appear on the estate but generally keep away from people. We brief everyone on what to do if you meet one.

How do I get there?

You can come by car, or by train or bus to Epe and then walk or take a taxi. After you sign up we send the route and help you find a ride-share if you like.

Can I book this as a business expense?

Yes. If you register through your company or as self-employed, we invoice your business. Add the company name and invoice address in the registration form.

Another question? Write to info@livinginconnectionretreat.nl and we will answer personally.
