Please read before you register

Terms and conditions

These terms apply to the Living in Connection nature retreat on 21 to 23 May 2027 at Landgoed Welna in Epe. You accept them when you submit the registration form.

Organiser, registration and payment

The Living in Connection nature retreat is organised by Rick de Gaay Fortman and Caroline Hamrit.

  • Your registration is complete once you have submitted the registration form and we have confirmed your place by email.
  • Private participants pay €645 for registrations before 1 May 2027 and €745 after that. These prices include 21% VAT. Business participants pay €595 before 1 May 2027 and €695 after that, plus 21% VAT, which is shown separately on the invoice. Business means that you register through your company or as self-employed, and the invoice is made out to your business. The price includes the camping fees, the guided activities and practices, and food and drinks. Travel to and from Epe and your own camping gear are not included.
  • After we confirm your place, you receive an invoice by email. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer or iDEAL, and in any case before 1 May 2027 to get the early bird price. Your place is guaranteed once payment has been received. For registrations after 1 May 2027, payment is due within 3 days.

Participants

The nature retreat will go ahead with a minimum of 5 participants and a maximum of 10 participants. The organisation can cancel the event if there are too few registrations. If that is the case, you will be informed as soon as possible and your payment will be reimbursed within a few days.

Safety and risk declaration

Participants choose to take part in the Living in Connection nature retreat at their own risk. In the case of an accident, participants will not hold the organisation accountable for any physical or non-physical damage that may occur as a result, in whatever way. This does not apply in case of serious carelessness or ill purpose on behalf of the organisation.

Participants declare that they are in sufficient physical and mental health to take part in the retreat. They will not hold the organisation accountable in any way for accidents or damage during the retreat that are a direct result of insufficient physical and/or mental health. Participants choose to take part at their own risk and take full responsibility for their own health.

Cancellation

If a participant cancels his or her participation in the nature retreat, the following cancellation conditions apply:

100%

Full refund

Cancellation up to 8 weeks before the first day of the event.

50%

Half refund

Cancellation between 4 and 8 weeks before the first day of the event.

0%

No refund

Cancellation within 4 weeks of the event.

Programme and weather

The retreat takes place outdoors. The organisers may change the programme, schedule or location when weather, safety or other circumstances call for it. If the retreat cannot go ahead because of circumstances beyond our control, we will offer a new date or a refund.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide complete and truthful information on the registration form, in particular about medical conditions and dietary needs.
  • Follow the instructions of the guides, especially on safety.
  • Treat the other participants, the guides and the nature of Landgoed Welna with respect and leave no trace.
  • The organisers may ask a participant to leave when their behaviour endangers or seriously disturbs others, without a refund.

Insurance

Participants are responsible for their own insurance, such as health, travel and accident cover. We recommend checking that your insurance covers outdoor activities.

Privacy and medical information

We use your personal details, including health information, only to organise and run the retreat and to keep you safe. We do not share them with third parties other than the guides and, in an emergency, medical services.

Photos and recordings

We only take photos or recordings of participants for promotion with their permission, and only publish them with that permission.

Replacing a participant

If you cannot attend, you may propose a replacement participant before the retreat starts, in agreement with the organisers. Cancelling is done by email to info@livinginconnectionretreat.nl, and the date of that email determines the refund.

Applicable law

Dutch law applies to these terms. Disputes go to the competent court in the Netherlands.
